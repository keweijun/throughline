Quantum Mechanics I — Problem set 1

Wavefunctions and superposition
1. A particle in $0 \le x \le L$ has $\psi(x) = A\,x(L-x)$ and $\psi = 0$ elsewhere. (a) Find $A$. (b) Find $\langle x\rangle$ without integrating, and explain why. (c) What is the probability of finding the particle in $0 \le x \le L/4$?
2. For the state of Problem 1, find $\langle p\rangle$ and $\langle p^2\rangle$.
3. Two paths to a detector have amplitudes $a = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$ and $b = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}e^{i\phi}$, up to a common factor. (a) Find the relative probability $P(\phi)$. (b) For which $\phi$ is the detector dark? (c) A detector placed on path 2 records every particle that passes. What is $P(\phi)$ now?
4. Electrons with kinetic energy $50\ \text{eV}$ pass through two slits $d = 1.0\ \mu\text{m}$ apart. Estimate the fringe spacing on a screen $L = 1.0\ \text{m}$ away.
5. Show that multiplying $\psi_1 + \psi_2$ by $e^{i\gamma}$ changes no probability, but multiplying only $\psi_2$ by $e^{i\gamma}$ does.
6. Verify $[\hat x,\hat p] = i\hbar$ by acting on a test function, and use it to evaluate $[\hat x^2,\hat p]$.
7. A state is $\psi = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\varphi_1 + \sqrt{\tfrac23}\,\varphi_2$ in the infinite square well. (a) What energies can a measurement give, and with what probabilities? (b) Find $\langle H\rangle$. (c) Is the state stationary?
8. Check that the Gaussian of Example 3.1 satisfies $\sigma_x\sigma_p = \hbar/2$.
9. An electron is confined to a well of width $L = 0.5\ \text{nm}$. Find the wavelength of the photon emitted in the $2 \to 1$ transition.
